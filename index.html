Helpful answers

Questions about getting powered?

What size of power station do I need?

It depends on combined running wattage, runtime and the product's continuous AC output. Use the calculator as a starting point and confirm against verified product specifications.

Can a power station run a refrigerator?

Possibly, but refrigerator compressors can draw a higher startup surge. Check continuous and surge ratings against the refrigerator nameplate before purchasing.

How long will an iTel PowerGo Pro last?

Runtime depends on verified usable battery capacity, inverter efficiency and connected load. Contact us for current product specifications.

What is the difference between Wh and W?

Watts (W) measure power draw. Watt-hours (Wh) measure energy over time. A 100 W load running for 3 hours uses about 300 Wh.

Can I charge a power station with solar panels?

Some models support solar charging. Compatibility depends on the station's solar input voltage, current and power limits. Check the exact product manual.

How much does a home solar installation cost?

It depends on system size, inverter, battery, panels, mounting, cabling and installation conditions. We provide a personalized quotation.
